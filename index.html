How We Work

Behind every shot.

Every project starts before we ever arrive on site. We review the location, check airspace, plan the flight path, and figure out which rig makes sense for the job. By the time we show up, we already know how we're going to shoot it.

On shoot day we typically run two passes — one to understand the space, one to capture it. Interior flythroughs are flown in first-person through goggles, usually 2–3 practice runs before we lock the final take. Exterior aerials and photography happen alongside, timed around the light. Most shoots are wrapped in under two hours.

After the shoot, footage goes straight into edit — color graded, cut to scope, and delivered the same day for most real estate projects. The goal every time is footage that actually gets used, not footage that sits in a folder.

The Gear

What we fly.

Ducted Cinewhoop

Built for tight spaces.

Our ducted cinewhoop is a small, fully enclosed FPV drone purpose-built for indoor use. The propeller guards keep it safe around people, furniture, and narrow doorways — letting us fly through spaces that standard drones simply can't enter. It moves at a natural walking pace through rooms, hallways, and staircases in a single unbroken take. No cuts. No resets. Just one continuous move from the front door through every room.

This is the drone responsible for the FPV flythroughs that make listings and venues look the way they actually feel to walk through.

DJI Drone

Built for altitude.

Our standard DJI drone handles everything outdoors — wide aerial shots, sweeping orbits around buildings, high-resolution HDR photography, and smooth cinematic altitude footage. GPS-stabilized and whisper-quiet, it's the right tool for weddings, events, and any exterior property work where you need precision and silence.

Every real estate package and wedding shoot that includes aerial photography runs on this rig. It captures angles no ground camera ever could.

Terms of Service.

Agreement

Acceptance of Terms

By booking a shoot, submitting payment, or paying a deposit, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service in full. These terms apply to all projects including real estate, weddings, events, commercial productions, and any custom scope agreed upon between the client and Michael FPV.

Payment

Deposits & Payment Structure

Certain projects require a non-refundable deposit to secure your shoot date and block time on our calendar. The deposit amount will be communicated at the time of booking and may range from 25% to 50% of the total project cost depending on scope and timeline.

The remaining balance is due upon delivery of final files, or as otherwise agreed in writing. Failure to complete payment does not entitle the client to retain any delivered assets.

Refunds

Non-Refundable Policy

All payments made to Michael FPV are non-refundable. This includes deposits, partial payments, and full balances. Once payment is submitted, it cannot be reversed regardless of whether the shoot has taken place.

This policy exists because time is blocked, preparation is done, and in many cases equipment and travel are arranged well in advance of your scheduled shoot date.

Client Responsibility

No Refund for Client Errors or No-Shows

No refund, credit, or reshoot will be issued in the following situations:

  • —The client does not show up to the scheduled shoot
  • —The client provides an incorrect address, wrong location, or incomplete access information
  • —The client is not ready at the agreed shoot time and the session cannot be completed
  • —The client cancels without adequate notice (less than 48 hours before the shoot)
  • —The client changes the scope, location, or requirements after the shoot has been completed
  • —The client is unsatisfied with the creative result but the agreed deliverables were provided

It is the client's full responsibility to provide accurate booking information, confirm the shoot address, and be present or ensure access is available at the agreed time.

Our Responsibility

When Michael FPV Is at Fault

If a project is not delivered due to an error, technical failure, or oversight on our part, Michael FPV will offer one of the following at our discretion:

  • —A reshoot at no additional cost to the client
  • —Re-editing or adjusting delivered footage if the issue is in post-production
  • —A partial credit toward a future booking

No monetary refund will be issued under any circumstance. Our commitment is to make it right through the work itself — not through reimbursement.

Rescheduling

Date Changes

Rescheduling requests must be made at least 48 hours before the original shoot date. One reschedule is permitted per booking at no charge, subject to availability. Additional reschedules may incur a rebooking fee. Rescheduling does not entitle the client to a refund of any deposit or payment already made.

Delivery

Delivery Timelines

Standard delivery timelines are as follows: real estate and commercial shoots within 24–48 hours; events within 3–5 business days; weddings within 5–7 business days. Timelines are estimates and may vary based on project complexity. Rush delivery may be available at an additional charge.

Licensing

Usage Rights

Upon receipt of full payment, the client receives a non-exclusive license to use all delivered footage and photos for marketing, MLS listings, social media, paid advertising, and general commercial use. Michael FPV retains the right to use project footage in portfolio, showreel, and promotional materials unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Compliance

FAA & Airspace

All drone operations are conducted by an FAA Part 107 certified pilot and comply with applicable federal, state, and local regulations. The client is responsible for obtaining any required venue permits or property permissions prior to the shoot date. Michael FPV reserves the right to cancel or modify a shoot if airspace restrictions, weather conditions, or safety concerns prevent safe flight — in which case a reschedule will be offered.
